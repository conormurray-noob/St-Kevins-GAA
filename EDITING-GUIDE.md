# How to Edit the St. Kevin's Website — Plain-English Guide

You do not need any technical knowledge to run this website. Everything is
edited through the **admin panel** — a set of simple forms.

## Getting in

- **Address:** `<your site>/admin/` (while testing: http://localhost:5173/admin/index.html)
- Log in with your email and password (you'll get an invite email when you're
  added as an editor).

## The golden rule

Find the section in the left-hand menu → change the fields → press **Publish**.
The website updates itself about a minute later. You can't break anything —
every change is saved with history and can be undone.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Common jobs, step by step

**Post the lotto results (2 minutes):**
1. Click **🎟 Club Lotto** → "Lotto Page"
2. Update "Current Jackpot" and "Next Draw"
3. Under "Draw Results", click **Add draw** and drag it to the top
4. Fill in date, the 4 numbers, jackpot, and the lucky dip winners
5. **Publish**

**Post a match report:**
1. Click **📰 News** → **New News Article**
2. Headline, date, category, upload a photo, short summary, then the report
   (empty line between paragraphs)
3. **Publish** — it appears on the News page instantly (the preview on the
   right shows exactly how it will look)

**Put a training session on the calendar:** it's automatic — anything in
🏋️ Training Schedule repeats every week. One-off sessions go in 📅 Calendar
Events instead.

## Photos

Wherever you see an image field, click it → **Upload** → choose from your
phone/computer. Landscape photos look best. The placeholder images (dark with
the club crest) are there to be replaced.

## Need help?

The site is maintained with the help of Claude (the club's AI pair of hands).
Anything structural — new pages, new features, design changes — ask the
website admin to request it in a Claude Code session.
